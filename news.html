New Album 'Shoot Me Your Ace' Climbs UK Charts

January 15, 2026

Glastonbury rockers REEF have made a triumphant return to the UK Top 20 with their newly released album 'Shoot Me Your Ace'. The album, produced by legendary Andy Taylor (Duran Duran/The Power Station), showcases the band's reenergised sound and confident spirit.

The band's evolution is evident throughout the album, delivering powerful rock anthems that resonate with both longtime fans and new listeners. 'Shoot Me Your Ace' is now available on all major streaming platforms and physical formats.
